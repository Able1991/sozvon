Start in 3 steps

  1. Download

    Open Sozvon.dmg and drag Sozvon to Applications.

  2. The setup does the rest

    It downloads the speech model and asks for access to the microphone, screen recording and system audio.

  3. Press the capsule

    When a call starts, press Record on the floating panel. After the call, the text is on your Mac.
